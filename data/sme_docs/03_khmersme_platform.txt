The KhmerSME Digital Platform

KhmerSME is a digital platform developed under the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation to serve as a one-stop information and service hub for Cambodian small and medium enterprises. The platform provides business information resources, guidance on registration and licensing, directories of SMEs and service providers, e-learning materials, and news about training programs, trade fairs, and government support schemes.

The goal of KhmerSME is to reduce the information gap that prevents many small business owners from accessing government services and growth opportunities. Instead of traveling to ministry offices, an entrepreneur can use the platform to learn which permits their business needs, find financing programs offered by partner institutions, list their products in an online directory, and follow step-by-step guides in the Khmer language. The platform also supports the government's broader digital economy agenda by encouraging SMEs to build an online presence and adopt digital tools in their daily operations.
