Export Procedures and Market Access for SMEs

Cambodian SMEs seeking to export must complete several steps: registering as an exporter, obtaining a certificate of origin from the Ministry of Commerce to claim preferential tariffs, meeting the product standards and sanitary requirements of the destination market, and clearing customs through the automated customs data system. Certificates of origin matter because Cambodia enjoys preferential access to major markets, including duty-free treatment for most goods under schemes for least developed countries and free trade agreements within ASEAN and with partners such as China and South Korea.

The Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership further expands preferential access across the Asia-Pacific. Practical barriers for small exporters include the cost of certification and laboratory testing, minimum volume requirements from buyers, packaging and labeling standards, and logistics costs. Trade promotion bodies and development programs assist SMEs through export readiness training, participation in international trade fairs, business matching with foreign buyers, and online marketplaces that let small producers reach regional customers directly.
