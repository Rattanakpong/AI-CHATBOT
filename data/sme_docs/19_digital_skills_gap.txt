The Digital Skills Gap in Cambodian Small Business

A persistent obstacle to SME digitization in Cambodia is the digital skills gap. Many business owners, particularly older entrepreneurs and those in rural provinces, have limited experience with computers, spreadsheets, or business software, even as smartphone use has become nearly universal. Digital adoption in small firms therefore tends to start with social media and messaging apps, especially Facebook and Telegram, used for marketing and taking orders, and with QR code payments, while accounting, inventory, and customer records often remain on paper.

Closing the gap involves several fronts: basic digital literacy training for business owners, in Khmer and oriented to practical business tasks; vocational and higher education programs producing graduates with information technology skills; and easy-to-use, Khmer-language business applications that lower the entry barrier. Government e-learning platforms, ministry training programs, chambers of commerce, and development partners all run digital upskilling courses for SMEs, covering topics from online selling and digital marketing to cybersecurity basics and simple bookkeeping applications.
