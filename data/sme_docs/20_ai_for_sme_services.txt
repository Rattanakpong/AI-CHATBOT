Artificial Intelligence in Government Services for SMEs

Artificial intelligence is beginning to appear in the delivery of government services to Cambodian businesses. Chatbots and AI assistants can answer common questions about registration procedures, licensing requirements, tax deadlines, and support programs in the Khmer language, at any time of day, without requiring a visit to a ministry office. Retrieval-based AI systems are particularly suited to this task: instead of inventing answers, they search a curated knowledge base of laws, procedures, and official guidance and respond with information grounded in those documents.

Building AI services for Khmer speakers presents specific technical challenges. Khmer script is written without spaces between words, which complicates text segmentation, and Khmer is a lower-resource language, with less training data available than for English. Careful data curation, question-answer datasets built from real citizen inquiries, and human review of AI responses are essential for quality. When designed well, AI assistants can dramatically widen access to government information for small business owners in remote provinces.
