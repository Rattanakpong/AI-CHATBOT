Food Safety and Product Standards for Small Producers

Food-producing SMEs in Cambodia must meet safety and quality requirements overseen by several institutions. The Institute of Standards of Cambodia, under the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation, develops national standards and manages product certification, while other agencies handle food inspection at markets and borders. A food safety law consolidates responsibilities across ministries covering production, processing, distribution, and sale.

For a small food producer, the practical journey usually involves registering the enterprise, ensuring premises and processes follow hygiene requirements, obtaining product registration or certification where required, and using compliant labels that list ingredients, production dates, and producer information in Khmer. Certifications such as good hygiene practice and good manufacturing practice signal quality to buyers, and standards compliance is essential for selling to supermarkets, hotels, and export markets. Support programs help SMEs upgrade facilities, train staff in hygiene, test products in accredited laboratories, and prepare documentation, because certification costs and technical complexity are significant hurdles for micro and small businesses.
