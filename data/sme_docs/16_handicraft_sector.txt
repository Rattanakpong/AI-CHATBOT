The Handicraft Sector and Artisan Businesses

Handicrafts occupy a special place in Cambodia's SME landscape, combining cultural heritage with livelihoods for rural artisans. Traditional products include silk weaving, silverwork, stone and wood carving, rattan and bamboo goods, ceramics, and the woven cotton scarf known as the krama. Many artisan businesses are family workshops or village cooperatives, and the sector employs a high proportion of women.

Handicraft enterprises register with the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation, which maintains a handicraft establishment registration distinct from factory permits. Common challenges are competition from cheap imported imitations, inconsistent quality, difficulty reaching buyers beyond local markets, and the aging of master artisans without successors. Support efforts focus on design improvement, quality grading, geographic indication and branding protection for distinctive products, e-commerce channels that let artisans sell directly to tourists and international customers, and integration with the tourism industry through workshop visits and craft markets in destinations such as Siem Reap.
