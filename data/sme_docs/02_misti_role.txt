The Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation

The Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation (MISTI) is the Cambodian government ministry responsible for industrial development, science and technology policy, innovation promotion, and the regulation of small and medium enterprises in the manufacturing sector. MISTI issues factory and handicraft establishment permits, oversees industrial standards and metrology, manages clean water supply regulation, and supports technology transfer to local businesses.

For SMEs, MISTI's General Department of SMEs and Handicrafts is the primary point of contact. It registers manufacturing SMEs and handicraft businesses, provides technical guidance on production processes and product quality, and runs programs that connect small producers with training, finance, and markets. MISTI also leads national initiatives in science, technology, and innovation policy, including Cambodia's STI roadmaps, which set priorities for building research capacity, developing digital infrastructure, and encouraging technology adoption across priority sectors such as agro-industry, manufacturing, and the digital economy.
