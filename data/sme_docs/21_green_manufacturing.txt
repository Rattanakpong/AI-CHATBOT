Green Manufacturing and Sustainable SME Practices

Sustainability is an emerging agenda for Cambodian industry. Green manufacturing for SMEs means using energy, water, and raw materials more efficiently, reducing waste and pollution, and adopting cleaner production technologies. Practical measures include switching to energy-efficient motors and LED lighting, installing solar panels to offset electricity costs, recycling production waste such as rice husks into fuel or animal feed, treating wastewater, and redesigning packaging to reduce plastic use.

Beyond environmental benefits, green practices increasingly affect market access: international buyers and brands sourcing from Cambodia ask suppliers about energy use, chemical management, and labor conditions, and some export markets are introducing carbon-related import rules. Financing programs have designated green investments as priority lending categories, and cleaner production programs help factories audit their resource use and identify savings that often pay for themselves. For small producers, the message is that efficiency upgrades cut monthly costs while positioning the business for supply chains that reward sustainability.
