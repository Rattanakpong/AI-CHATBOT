Techo Startup Center and the Startup Ecosystem

Techo Startup Center is a government initiative under the Ministry of Economy and Finance that incubates and accelerates Cambodian startups, with a focus on digital technology ventures. It runs structured programs that help founders validate ideas, build products, and connect with investors, and it develops shared digital infrastructure that startups and SMEs can build upon, including platforms for digital identity, data exchange, and business services.

The center is part of a wider ecosystem supporting entrepreneurship in Cambodia that includes university incubators, private accelerators, co-working spaces, and development partner programs. Priority areas have included fintech, agritech, edtech, and logistics technology, reflecting sectors where digital solutions can address clear Cambodian market needs. For SMEs, the startup ecosystem matters as a source of locally built digital tools, such as delivery platforms, inventory apps, and payment services designed for the Cambodian market and available in the Khmer language, which lowers the barrier for traditional businesses to digitize their operations.
