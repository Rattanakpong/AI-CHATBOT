Tourism SMEs and the Visitor Economy

Tourism is a major pillar of the Cambodian economy, anchored by the Angkor temple complex in Siem Reap, the coastal areas around Sihanoukville and Kampot, and the capital Phnom Penh. The visitor economy is served overwhelmingly by small businesses: guesthouses, family restaurants, tour agencies, tuk-tuk and transport operators, souvenir shops, massage and wellness providers, and craft workshops.

Tourism SMEs were severely affected by the collapse of international travel during the pandemic years, and recovery efforts emphasized upgrading quality, diversifying attractions beyond the temples, and digitizing the sector. Small operators are encouraged to list on online booking platforms, accept QR code payments, maintain active social media pages, and collect online reviews. Government financing schemes designated tourism as a priority sector for concessional loans, helping small operators renovate and reopen. Community-based tourism, in which villages host visitors for homestays, crafts, and nature activities, spreads tourism income to rural areas and creates markets for local food producers and artisans.
