Intellectual Property Protection for Small Businesses

Intellectual property protection helps Cambodian SMEs defend their brands and creations. Trademarks, which protect business names, logos, and product marks, are registered with the Department of Intellectual Property of the Ministry of Commerce; registration gives the owner exclusive rights and the ability to act against imitators. Patents and industrial designs for inventions and product appearances are handled by the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation, while copyright covers creative works.

Cambodia also protects geographic indications, which certify that a product's qualities come from its place of origin. Kampot pepper and Kampong Speu palm sugar are flagship registered geographic indications whose producers can command premium prices in export markets. For a typical SME, the most immediately valuable step is trademark registration: as a business builds a reputation, an unregistered brand can be copied or even registered by someone else. Registration procedures have been progressively digitized, and small businesses can seek assistance from the responsible departments or private IP agents.
