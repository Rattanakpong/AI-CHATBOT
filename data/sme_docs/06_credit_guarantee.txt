The Credit Guarantee Corporation of Cambodia

The Credit Guarantee Corporation of Cambodia (CGCC) is a state-owned enterprise created to help businesses that lack sufficient collateral obtain loans. Instead of lending money itself, the CGCC guarantees a portion of loans issued by participating financial institutions. If a guaranteed borrower defaults, the CGCC compensates the lender for the guaranteed share, which reduces the lender's risk and makes banks more willing to lend to smaller businesses.

Collateral is a critical obstacle for Cambodian SMEs: banks traditionally demand land titles as security, which many small entrepreneurs, women-owned businesses, and young companies do not have. Under a credit guarantee scheme, a qualifying business can borrow with little or no hard collateral because the guarantee substitutes for it. Guarantee schemes have covered working capital loans, investment loans, and financing for priority sectors, with dedicated schemes for women entrepreneurs. Borrowers pay a modest guarantee fee, and loans must still be assessed as commercially viable by the lending institution.
