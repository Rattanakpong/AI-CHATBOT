The SME Bank of Cambodia

The SME Bank of Cambodia is a state-owned bank established by the Royal Government of Cambodia to improve access to affordable financing for small and medium enterprises. It operates both directly and through co-financing schemes, in which the SME Bank partners with participating commercial banks and microfinance institutions to share the lending risk and lower interest rates for qualifying borrowers.

Priority sectors for SME Bank financing have included food and beverage manufacturing, agro-processing, waste recycling, tourism-related enterprises, and businesses producing goods that substitute for imports. Loan products typically offer lower interest rates and longer repayment terms than standard commercial loans, and some schemes reduce collateral requirements, which is one of the biggest barriers Cambodian SMEs face when borrowing. To qualify, a business generally needs to be formally registered, demonstrate a viable business plan, and fall within the size definitions for small and medium enterprises. The bank complements other government financing vehicles such as credit guarantee schemes.
