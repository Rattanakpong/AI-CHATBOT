Business Registration for Cambodian SMEs

Business registration in Cambodia has been progressively digitized through the Online Business Registration platform, a single portal that links several ministries. Through this system, an entrepreneur can register a company with the Ministry of Commerce, obtain a tax identification from the General Department of Taxation, and apply for relevant permits from other ministries in one consolidated process. Registration that once required weeks of visits to separate offices can now be completed online in a matter of days.

Formal registration brings concrete benefits to SMEs. Registered businesses can open corporate bank accounts, issue proper invoices, participate in government procurement, apply for bank loans and government financing schemes, and join official support programs. Sole proprietorships are the simplest and most common structure for micro and small businesses, while private limited companies are preferred by growing firms that want limited liability and the ability to take on investors. Manufacturing businesses must additionally register with the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation.
