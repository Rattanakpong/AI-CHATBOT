Women Entrepreneurs in the Cambodian SME Sector

Women play an outsized role in Cambodian small business: a majority of the country's micro and small enterprises are owned or operated by women, concentrated in retail trade, food production, tailoring, beauty services, and handicrafts. Despite this, women-owned businesses tend to be smaller, less likely to be formally registered, and less likely to obtain bank financing than businesses owned by men, partly because women more often lack land titles to pledge as collateral.

Targeted support has therefore become a policy priority. Credit guarantee schemes have included dedicated windows for women entrepreneurs, offering higher guarantee coverage so banks can lend with less collateral. Business associations such as the Cambodia Women Entrepreneurs Association provide networking, mentorship, and training. Development programs focus on digital skills for women-led businesses, helping owners use social media marketing, digital payments, and online bookkeeping. Improving conditions for women entrepreneurs is seen as one of the highest-leverage ways to raise SME productivity and household incomes nationwide.
