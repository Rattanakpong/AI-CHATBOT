Defining Small and Medium Enterprises in Cambodia

In Cambodia, small and medium enterprises (SMEs) are commonly classified by the number of employees and the value of assets, excluding land. Micro enterprises typically employ fewer than 10 people, small enterprises employ roughly 10 to 50 people, and medium enterprises employ roughly 51 to 100 people. Asset-based thresholds are also used, with small enterprises generally holding assets valued below about 250,000 US dollars and medium enterprises below about 500,000 US dollars, though exact figures vary by regulation and sector.

SMEs form the backbone of the Cambodian economy. They account for the overwhelming majority of registered businesses in the country and provide a large share of total employment, particularly in rural areas. Common SME activities include food and beverage processing, handicrafts, garments, retail trade, repair services, and agro-processing. Because most Cambodian SMEs are family-run and informally organized, government policy has focused on encouraging formal registration, improving access to finance, and building the technical and digital skills of owners and workers.
