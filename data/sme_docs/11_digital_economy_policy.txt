The Cambodia Digital Economy and Society Policy Framework

The Cambodia Digital Economy and Society Policy Framework 2021 to 2035 is the government's long-term roadmap for building a vibrant digital economy. It rests on the pillars of digital infrastructure, digital trust and security, digital citizens, digital government, and digital business. The framework sets out phased goals: laying foundations such as connectivity and digital skills, promoting adoption across the economy, and ultimately transforming Cambodia into a digitally driven society.

For businesses, the framework prioritizes digital adoption by SMEs, promotion of e-commerce, development of digital payment systems, and the growth of a local tech startup ecosystem. It recognizes that most Cambodian enterprises are micro, small, or medium sized, and that national digital transformation depends on these businesses adopting digital tools, from basic online presence and digital payments to e-commerce and enterprise software. Implementation involves multiple institutions, including ministries responsible for telecommunications, commerce, industry, and finance, along with dedicated committees coordinating the digital economy agenda.
