Cambodia's Industrial Development Policy

Cambodia's Industrial Development Policy set the direction for transforming the country's industrial base from labor-intensive, low-value activities toward a more diversified, skill-driven, and technology-based industry. Its goals included raising the industrial sector's share of gross domestic product, diversifying exports beyond garments into processed foods, machinery parts, and electronics, and promoting the modernization of small and medium enterprises.

Key measures relevant to SMEs include encouraging formal registration, improving access to industrial land and reliable electricity, developing industrial parks and special economic zones, strengthening technical and vocational training, and linking local suppliers to foreign investors and global value chains. The policy treats SMEs as essential to building domestic supply chains: when large factories can source inputs such as packaging, components, and processed materials from local small producers, more value stays in the Cambodian economy. Successor strategies continue this agenda with greater emphasis on automation, quality standards, innovation, and the transition to higher-technology manufacturing.
