Tax Obligations of Small Businesses in Cambodia

Cambodia operates a self-assessment tax regime administered by the General Department of Taxation, with taxpayers classified as small, medium, or large based on annual turnover and business form. Small taxpayers are typically sole proprietorships or partnerships whose annual turnover falls within thresholds set by regulation. They benefit from simplified accounting requirements and simplified tax rates compared to medium and large taxpayers.

Registered small businesses generally deal with several core taxes: an annual income tax or the minimum tax, monthly prepayment of income tax calculated on turnover, value-added tax for businesses above the VAT threshold, withholding taxes on certain payments, and the annual patent tax, a business license fee paid each year. Small taxpayers can use simplified bookkeeping, and the tax administration has introduced e-filing and mobile applications to make monthly declarations easier. Newly registered SMEs in certain priority sectors have been offered tax incentives, including income tax exemptions for a number of years, to encourage formalization.
